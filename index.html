I'm an AI researcher. I hold an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Koç University, where I did my research in GGLab with Prof. Gözde Gül Şahin. My work is about adapting large language and vision-language models efficiently: selective and parameter-efficient fine-tuning, model merging, and privacy-preserving training of LLMs with federated learning. Prior to my MSc, I earned a BSc in Electrical Engineering from Istanbul Technical University.

I also bring industry experience: three years as a data scientist at Hepsiburada, building segmentation, churn, lifetime-value and recommendation models for an e-commerce platform with over 70 million customers. I later worked as a senior AI consultant for the Data & AI team at Badael in Riyadh. Earlier, at Baykar's Computer Vision R&D department, I developed real-time object detection and visual tracking models for autonomous UAV platforms.

Research

MSc thesis · 2026

SWIFT: Selective Weight Identification and Fine Tuning

A selective parameter-efficient fine-tuning framework that identifies and updates only the weights that matter for a new task, which reduces catastrophic forgetting and cuts training compute. An extension adds a merging mechanism that prevents parameter interference when combining tasks.
